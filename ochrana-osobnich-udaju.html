What we process and why

Booking an appointment. Name, phone, email, chosen service, barber, time and any note. We need them to book your appointment, remind you of it and change or cancel it if needed. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Kept for 12 months after your last appointment.

Gift cards. Recipient name, who the card is from, the message, delivery email, card type and value, and payment details. Used to issue, deliver and redeem the card. Legal basis: performance of a contract and, for sales records, a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). Card details are kept while the card is valid and for 12 months after; accounting records for the period set by accounting and tax law, up to 10 years. You enter card details directly with the payment gateway; we never see or store them.

When you contact us. Your message and the contact you use. Needed so we can reply. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Kept for 12 months.

Visitor statistics. Only if you agree in the cookie bar. Google Analytics 4 then records which pages you view, on what device and where you came from. Google Analytics does not store IP addresses. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with Cookie settings in the footer. Kept for 14 months.

Running the website. The server hosting the site logs technical access data including IP address for security and abuse prevention. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Cookies

On its own the site uses only essential browser storage to remember your language, light or dark mode and your cookie choice. Google Analytics cookies (named _ga, lasting up to 2 years) are set only after you consent. We use no advertising cookies.

Who else handles your data

We share data only with those who help us provide the service, and only as far as needed: the Slotio booking system, the payment gateway, Google (email and Google Analytics), GitHub (website hosting) and our accountant. All of them process data on our instructions. We never sell your data.

Google and GitHub may process data in the USA. Both are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has found to provide adequate protection.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest and to withdraw consent at any time. Just email noarprague@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

If you believe we are not handling your data properly, you can complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, uoou.gov.cz.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

Validity
